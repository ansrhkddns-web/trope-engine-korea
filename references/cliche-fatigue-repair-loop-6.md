# Cliche Fatigue Repair Loop 6

Use this file with `reader-feedback-calibrator-6.md` when a cliche portfolio risks becoming stale, repetitive, too familiar, too easy, or untrustworthy across episodes.

Phase 6 treats fatigue as a design signal, not a reason to throw away the genre promise. Keep the familiar reward, then change the condition under which the reward is earned.

## Fatigue Types

| Fatigue type | Reader feeling | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| proof fatigue | "또 시험/랭킹/결투네" | same proof object repeats | rotate proof object: audit, rescue, contract, broadcast, auction, hearing, duel |
| witness fatigue | "또 주변이 놀라기만 하네" | same reaction group repeats | rotate witness authority: rival, institution, enemy, beneficiary, public, private intimate |
| reward fatigue | "계속 인정만 받네" | one reward channel dominates | add money, access, safety, revenge progress, relationship, knowledge, territory |
| cost fatigue | "억지로 막히네" | random punishment after payoff | make cost caused by the protagonist's win |
| antagonist fatigue | "악역이 너무 멍청하다" | opposition exists only to be humiliated | give antagonist correct partial information and real leverage |
| novelty fatigue | "말만 새롭고 똑같다" | terminology changes but pressure stays same | change institution, metric, method, consequence, or moral frame |
| lore fatigue | "설명은 많은데 장면이 없다" | worldbuilding arrives before proof | convert lore into test, hearing, gate, board meeting, duel, or contract |
| hook fatigue | "또 낚시네" | hook withholds instead of paying | pay one promise before opening the next pressure |

## Repair Operators

Apply one or two operators at a time.

| Operator | Use when | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| proof rotation | same payoff scene repeats | change the public measurement form |
| witness escalation | reaction feels small | upgrade witness authority or emotional relevance |
| cost causality | pressure feels arbitrary | make the new threat a direct consequence of the win |
| competence injection | antagonist feels weak | let the opponent correctly infer one part of the protagonist's method |
| reward diversification | payoff feels narrow | add a second reward channel |
| debt payoff | reader patience is strained | answer one old question before opening a new one |
| institution turn | world feels static | make a guild, board, sect, school, court, system, or media body change policy |
| moral pressure | power fantasy feels flat | force a choice between two rewards, not good versus stupid |
| scale echo | long-term arc feels unrelated | repeat the same reward at a larger institution level |
| copy-shadow break | sequence resembles a named work | change institution, proof object, action order, and consequence |

## Freshness Budget

Use only enough novelty to refresh the familiar promise.

| Budget | Use for | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-axis repair | quick pack, 1화 proof | change one of proof, witness, cost, or institution |
| 2-axis repair | 1-5 episode loop | change proof plus cost, or witness plus institution |
| 3-axis repair | bible or long-running engine | change proof, institution, and antagonist logic |

If more than three axes change at once, re-check whether the genre promise is still recognizable.

## 1-5 Episode Anti-Fatigue Pattern

Use this pattern after selecting a portfolio.

| Episode | Familiar reward | Changed pressure | Repair operator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | first visible proof | immediate survival/status pressure | proof rotation |
| 2 | method repeat | different rule or terrain | cost causality |
| 3 | social recognition | institutional audit or ranking | witness escalation |
| 4 | stronger opponent | antagonist uses partial truth | competence injection |
| 5 | mini-arc payoff | paid-boundary larger question | debt payoff |

## Genre-Specific Fatigue Repairs

| Genre | Common fatigue | Best repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hunter/gate | repeated raid clear | switch from clear time to casualty audit, rescue evidence, ownership hearing, broadcast backlash |
| tower/system | repeated quest exploit | add rule patch, sponsor reaction, penalty conversion, rival interpretation |
| regression/finance | omniscient future wins | require due diligence, timeline drift, regulator proof, opponent counter-buy |
| chaebol/corporate | boardroom victory repeats | rotate proof through shares, contracts, media, court, supply chain, labor risk |
| murim | duel after duel | rotate through medicine, formation, sect law, escort mission, honor debt |
| academy | exam repetition | use club audit, field trip, sponsor evaluation, disciplinary hearing, team project |
| possession/villain/extra | script knowledge solves all | add relationship debt, route mutation, social proof, legal constraint |
| support/profession | competence shown privately | make output measurable: survival rate, budget, item grade, patient result, contract |
| romance/rofan | misunderstanding loops | pay one emotional truth, then open status/legal/family pressure |

## Do Not

- Do not remove the familiar reward to look fresh.
- Do not fix repetition by adding terminology.
- Do not add a stronger enemy unless the enemy is connected to the last win.
- Do not make cost feel like punishment for reader satisfaction.
- Do not open a paid hook before paying at least one visible promise.
