# Trope Combination Matrix

Use this file when combining genres or when a concept feels like a pile of cool devices without a clear main promise.

## Combination Rule

Pick one primary engine and one support engine.

- **Primary engine** sells the click and owns the first proof.
- **Support engine** adds reward variety, setting pressure, or long-term expansion.
- If two engines both demand the first proof, choose the more market-facing promise and demote the other.
- Do not introduce a support engine until it strengthens the primary reward.

## Primary vs Support Roles

| Primary engine | What it owns | Good support engines | Support should add |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hunter/Gate | raid proof, rank, loot, rescue | finance, academy, regression, system | money logic, tests, hidden info, growth UI |
| Tower/System | rule exploitation, floors, titles | constellation, regression, martial arts, streaming | sponsor pressure, future knowledge, legitimacy conflict, public reaction |
| Regression | early mover advantage | chaebol, hunter, apocalypse, academy, martial arts | assets, disasters, recruit targets, known exams, lost manuals |
| Chaebol/Finance | money, family hierarchy, ownership | gate, regression, entertainment, legal revenge | commodities, timing, public image, evidence |
| Martial Arts/Sect | realm growth, duel, faction honor | system, regression, finance, academy | measurable growth, lost knowledge, resource economy, formal tests |
| Possession/Extra | death-flag avoidance, script disruption | academy, romance fantasy, hunter, martial arts | social pressure, route control, rank proof, faction survival |
| Academy/Ranking | tests, rivals, team, rank | hunter, system, chaebol, possession | stakes, measurable reward, sponsor politics, script knowledge |

## High-Utility Combinations

### Hunter + Finance

- **Core promise**: monster-era value is mispriced; protagonist profits and proves combat/social value.
- **First proof**: buys ignored gate-adjacent asset, then a gate incident proves why it matters.
- **Best rewards**: money, rank, public recognition, revenge.
- **Risk**: finance scenes become dry. Fix by tying every asset to a raid, rescue, public loss, or guild reaction.

### Regression + Chaebol

- **Core promise**: future knowledge lets protagonist take back family power.
- **First proof**: saves or flips one small subsidiary before a larger family conflict.
- **Best rewards**: revenge, money, status, autonomy.
- **Risk**: effortless prediction. Fix with legal proof, capital limits, family suspicion, changed future.

### Tower + Constellation

- **Core promise**: protagonist negotiates with rules and sponsors better than stronger challengers.
- **First proof**: clears tutorial by hidden condition and attracts unusual sponsor attention.
- **Best rewards**: title, sponsor reaction, public ranking, secret rule access.
- **Risk**: sponsor chatter has no teeth. Fix with contracts, debt, faction alignment, and sponsor consequences.

### Martial Arts + Finance

- **Core promise**: sect power is also resource control.
- **First proof**: wins a duel or faction dispute through medicine supply, debt, or manual ownership.
- **Best rewards**: realm growth, sect rank, money, ideological revenge.
- **Risk**: money weakens martial romance. Fix by making resources directly affect training, medicine, honor, and survival.

### Martial Arts + System

- **Core promise**: the new measurement collides with old legitimacy.
- **First proof**: system undervalues or mislabels a technique that an elder recognizes.
- **Best rewards**: power, recognition, rule conflict, hidden path.
- **Risk**: system solves martial effort. Fix by requiring duel evidence, body cost, and sect acknowledgment.

### Possession + Academy

- **Core promise**: protagonist survives a scripted role by changing social scenes.
- **First proof**: refuses the expected humiliation/death-flag event and causes a new relationship shift.
- **Best rewards**: survival, reputation repair, route disruption, hidden reward.
- **Risk**: original plot summary replaces action. Fix by making each known event become a test, scene, choice, or consequence.

### Gate + Real Estate

- **Core promise**: geography becomes power because gates change land value and safety.
- **First proof**: land everyone mocks becomes a strategic rescue/logistics site.
- **Best rewards**: money, rescue, public proof, family reversal.
- **Risk**: speculation feels detached. Fix with local civilians, guild access, disaster routes, and government reaction.

### Regression + Apocalypse/Base

- **Core promise**: protagonist prepares early, rescues selectively, and builds a survivable power base.
- **First proof**: one preparation pays off during a disaster others ignored.
- **Best rewards**: safety, leadership, knowledge, loyalty.
- **Risk**: hoarding fantasy becomes inert. Fix by forcing choices about who to save, what to reveal, and what future changes.

## Combination Conflict Checks

Before finalizing, answer:

- Which genre is visible in the first scene?
- Which genre owns the first reward?
- Does the support genre create scenes or only terminology?
- Are the reward ladders compatible?
- Is the protagonist's advantage useful in both engines?
- Does the institution that reacts belong to the primary engine or the support engine?
- Can this combination run for 50 episodes without switching promises?

## Repair Patterns

- **Too many devices**: choose one primary engine and delay the rest.
- **Weak first proof**: prove the primary genre first, then reveal support value.
- **Genre mismatch**: convert the support genre into an institution, market, rule, or witness.
- **No long-term ladder**: define personal enemy -> institution -> regional/national/global/heavenly scale.
- **No freshness**: change the proof object or institution, not the entire genre promise.
