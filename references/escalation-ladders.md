# Escalation Ladders

Use this file when generating episode seeds, 1-50 episode roadmaps, long-term engines, or when checking whether a trope pack can sustain serialization.

## Universal Ladder

| Range | Job | Required payoff | Required pressure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Episode 1 | Prove the genre promise. | one clear reward | one concrete next-click question |
| Episodes 2-5 | Prove repeatability. | repeated fun with variation | first rival/institution reaction |
| Episodes 6-10 | Add cost and social proof. | larger reward or public recognition | contract, debt, suspicion, stronger enemy |
| Episodes 11-25 | Build the first major ladder. | rank, asset, team, sect/family position | organization-level opposition |
| Episodes 26-50 | Expand the arena. | major arc victory | larger institution, region, sponsor, market, or faction |
| Episodes 51-100 | Reveal deeper engine. | origin clue, world-rule access, high-status win | national/global/heavenly/system-level response |
| 100+ | Rotate payoff and reinvention. | big reward followed by changed rules | new scale with same reader promise |

## Enemy Ladder

Use an enemy ladder to avoid random antagonist replacement.

1. **Personal blocker**: bully, rival, guild clerk, family member, senior disciple, classmate.
2. **Local institution**: guild team, department, sect hall, school faculty, subsidiary, floor clan.
3. **Major organization**: association, family board, orthodox alliance, demonic cult branch, national guild, tower administration.
4. **Public/national layer**: media, government, market, international raid body, imperial court.
5. **Hidden engine**: system origin, constellation economy, regressor cause, heavenly law, founding family secret.

Each step should reuse earlier conflicts at a larger scale. Do not discard the core pleasure.

## Institution Reaction Ladder

| Stage | Institution reaction | Useful scenes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ignore | protagonist is dismissed or mismeasured | rank test, board insult, outer disciple task |
| Notice | someone records an anomaly | scout offer, elder glance, audit note, sponsor message |
| Contest | institution tries to control or discredit | hearing, duel, contract trap, hostile bid |
| Depend | institution needs protagonist's value | rescue request, emergency raid, board vote, sect crisis |
| Fear | protagonist changes the institution's balance | reform, takeover, faction split, rule revision |
| Negotiate | protagonist becomes a power center | alliance, treaty, sponsorship war, succession bargain |

## Genre-Specific Ladders

### Hunter/Gate

- **1화**: rank/gate proof, rescue, or mismeasurement.
- **2-5화**: low-rank raids expose abnormal efficiency or hidden rule reading.
- **6-10화**: guilds, association, market, or media react.
- **11-25화**: first guild conflict, item economy, gate ownership, disaster response.
- **26-50화**: regional/national raid, hidden gate rule, major public proof.
- **50+**: gate origin, system/constellation layer, international politics.

### Tower/System

- **1화**: tutorial exploit or hidden condition.
- **2-5화**: titles, sponsor attention, ranking anomaly.
- **6-10화**: clan recruitment, contract pressure, penalty cost.
- **11-25화**: floor economy, administrator conflict, sponsor faction.
- **26-50화**: rule-breaking precedent, floor civilization, outside-world impact.
- **50+**: system origin, sponsor war, tower governance.

### Regression/Information

- **1화**: one future fact pays off visibly.
- **2-5화**: future knowledge creates assets, allies, or avoided losses.
- **6-10화**: timeline changes and memory becomes less absolute.
- **11-25화**: protagonist builds a network and faces suspicion.
- **26-50화**: known major disaster arrives differently.
- **50+**: why regression happened, who else knows, what future can no longer be trusted.

### Chaebol/Finance

- **1화**: tiny decisive deal, land, stock, or document proves value.
- **2-5화**: family hierarchy reacts, first asset accumulates.
- **6-10화**: board, bank, prosecutor, or media pressure.
- **11-25화**: subsidiary control, hostile bid, inheritance conflict.
- **26-50화**: national market, political tie, global capital.
- **50+**: economic order, state-level conflict, hidden family origin.

### Martial Arts/Sect

- **1화**: broken-body workaround, duel upset, manual insight, poison reversal.
- **2-5화**: training proof with public witness.
- **6-10화**: hall/elder/faction response and first debt.
- **11-25화**: sect tournament, medicine route, faction betrayal.
- **26-50화**: orthodox/demonic conflict, regional reputation.
- **50+**: imperial/heavenly order, origin of martial law, transcendent cost.

## Arc Generator

For each arc, define:

- **Arc promise**: what reward this arc pays.
- **Entry pressure**: why the protagonist must act now.
- **Trope card stack**: 3-5 cards used in order.
- **Midpoint proof**: public proof that changes social position.
- **Cost reveal**: what the win creates.
- **Final payoff**: visible reward.
- **Next arena**: what larger institution now reacts.

## Expansion Failure Signs

- The protagonist only grows numerically.
- Each new villain has no institutional relation to the last.
- The first genre promise disappears after the first arc.
- World mystery replaces episode rewards.
- The story expands scale but not choice, cost, witness, or consequence.

## Repair Moves

- Reconnect a new arc to the first reward type.
- Make the next enemy the owner, regulator, sponsor, creditor, teacher, or beneficiary of the last defeated enemy.
- Add a public proof scene at each scale jump.
- Reveal one deeper rule only after paying the current arc promise.
- Use a new arena to repeat the same pleasure in a bigger form.
