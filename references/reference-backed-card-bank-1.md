# Reference-Backed Card Bank 1

Use this file when the user asks for more diverse cliche references or when the existing Korean webnovel cards feel too narrow. These cards translate broad trope, motif, and story-function references into Korean webnovel-ready forms.

## How to Use

- Pick 3-7 cards for quick ideation.
- Pick 8-12 cards for a full concept engine.
- For each selected card, assign a scene job: first proof, reward loop, social proof, cost reveal, escalation, package hook, or long-term mystery.

## Cards

### Donor Test With Hidden Criterion

- **Reference function**: a gatekeeper tests the hero before aid is granted.
- **Korean webnovel form**: tutorial hidden condition, elder test, sponsor contract, guild audit, academy practical exam.
- **Reward**: recognition, power, secret access.
- **Proof object**: title, manual, contract, rank review, hidden reward.
- **Fresh variation**: the test measures rescue value, contract literacy, logistics, restraint, or moral cost instead of combat.
- **Failure mode**: aid is handed out without protagonist action.

### Recognition Token

- **Reference function**: a token proves identity, status, or rightful claim.
- **Korean webnovel form**: sect token, guild license, family seal, system title, share certificate, land deed, rescue footage.
- **Reward**: status, legitimacy, access.
- **Proof object**: the token itself.
- **Fresh variation**: the token is legally valid but socially dangerous.
- **Failure mode**: token changes nothing in the world.

### Forbidden Room / Sealed Access

- **Reference function**: forbidden access creates temptation, danger, and hidden truth.
- **Korean webnovel form**: sealed tower floor, restricted gate, family archive, forbidden manual vault, academy lab, medicine hall.
- **Reward**: knowledge, power, mystery.
- **Proof object**: key, log, manual, seal break, hidden map.
- **Fresh variation**: entry is allowed only through contract, debt, legal loophole, or public wager.
- **Failure mode**: the forbidden place is just a loot room.

### False Hero Exposure

- **Reference function**: a false claimant is exposed by true proof.
- **Korean webnovel form**: fake ranker, corrupt guild star, favored heir, orthodox hypocrite, fake original protagonist, rigged academy top student.
- **Reward**: revenge, recognition, moral satisfaction.
- **Proof object**: system log, audit ledger, duel evidence, rank replay, poison trace.
- **Fresh variation**: exposing the false hero damages an institution the protagonist still needs.
- **Failure mode**: false hero is obviously stupid from the start.

### Impossible Task

- **Reference function**: the hero is given a task meant to be impossible.
- **Korean webnovel form**: low-rank solo raid, impossible board vote, poison cure, manual interpretation, hostile acquisition, tower floor clear.
- **Reward**: competence, public proof, status.
- **Proof object**: clear message, cured patient, vote result, acquisition contract, duel record.
- **Fresh variation**: success condition is non-obvious and tied to the protagonist's support skill or future knowledge.
- **Failure mode**: impossible task is solved by raw power only.

### Taboo Contract

- **Reference function**: a prohibition or bargain gives power with a price.
- **Korean webnovel form**: system penalty clause, sponsor pact, guild slave contract, sect oath, family inheritance condition.
- **Reward**: power, access, tension.
- **Proof object**: contract line, vow scar, penalty timer, title restriction.
- **Fresh variation**: protagonist wins by interpreting the contract better than the institution.
- **Failure mode**: contract is ignored after the power-up.

### Pursuit After Proof

- **Reference function**: once value is proven, the hero is hunted or pursued.
- **Korean webnovel form**: association audit, guild suppression, family investigators, sect chase, media scandal, hostile fund attack.
- **Reward**: proof matters; scale increases.
- **Proof object**: warrant, audit letter, bounty, article, hostile bid.
- **Fresh variation**: pursuers are rational protectors of institutional risk, not cartoon villains.
- **Failure mode**: pursuit is random and disconnected from the reward.

### Deliverance Under Public Timer

- **Reference function**: rescue under threat.
- **Korean webnovel form**: gate break, tower penalty, debt deadline, poison countdown, board liquidation, execution duel.
- **Reward**: safety, competence, public reaction.
- **Proof object**: saved life, stopped timer, cured poison, delayed liquidation, rescue footage.
- **Fresh variation**: rescue also exposes mispricing, corruption, or hidden ownership.
- **Failure mode**: rescue has no witness or cost.

### Revolt Against Measuring System

- **Reference function**: revolt against tyrant or unjust order.
- **Korean webnovel form**: guild labor revolt, academy ranking challenge, sect faction split, shareholder coup, association rule appeal.
- **Reward**: autonomy, justice, status.
- **Proof object**: petition, vote, duel ruling, public ranking, proxy document.
- **Fresh variation**: protagonist does not destroy the system; they replace the measurement standard.
- **Failure mode**: revolt becomes speeches without scene proof.

### Daring Enterprise

- **Reference function**: bold quest for a high-value object under opposition.
- **Korean webnovel form**: impossible raid, forbidden-floor clear, relic theft, hostile takeover, rescue behind enemy lines.
- **Reward**: thrill, competence, acquisition.
- **Proof object**: artifact, deed, contract, rescued person, title.
- **Fresh variation**: the object is valuable because of later institutional reaction, not only power.
- **Failure mode**: quest is just bigger combat.

### Motif Ownership Twist

- **Reference function**: a motif is not free; it belongs to someone or some law.
- **Korean webnovel form**: manual has owner, land has hidden lien, gate resource has government claim, title has sponsor debt, medicine recipe has sect taboo.
- **Reward**: depth, consequence, long-term pressure.
- **Proof object**: ownership record, debt ledger, claim notice, old seal.
- **Fresh variation**: protagonist must decide whether to reveal ownership or exploit silence.
- **Failure mode**: ownership is trivia with no plot effect.

### Reversed Donor

- **Reference function**: the helper tests the hero; here the hero tests the helper.
- **Korean webnovel form**: protagonist evaluates a sponsor, guild, elder, investor, professor, or constellation before accepting aid.
- **Reward**: autonomy, intelligence, status.
- **Proof object**: refused contract, revised clause, public rejection, counteroffer.
- **Fresh variation**: refusing the obvious strongest helper unlocks a weaker but better-aligned route.
- **Failure mode**: refusal is attitude, not strategy.

### False Curse / Hidden Compatibility

- **Reference function**: misfortune is reinterpreted as special condition.
- **Korean webnovel form**: broken meridian, F-rank, cursed title, failed heir, low compatibility, support class.
- **Reward**: reversal, power, identity.
- **Proof object**: alternate measurement, medicine result, hidden class, duel effect, crafting output.
- **Fresh variation**: the "curse" is still costly and socially dangerous.
- **Failure mode**: curse simply becomes blessing.

### Evidence Before Revenge

- **Reference function**: vengeance requires proof and public venue.
- **Korean webnovel form**: boardroom exposure, sect trial, guild hearing, academy disciplinary meeting, association audit.
- **Reward**: catharsis, justice, social proof.
- **Proof object**: ledger, system log, witness, poison trace, contract contradiction.
- **Fresh variation**: partial evidence wins a small procedural battle first.
- **Failure mode**: revenge is delayed without progress markers.

### Recognition Delayed By Wrong Audience

- **Reference function**: true value is seen by the wrong or powerless person first.
- **Korean webnovel form**: ordinary civilian, junior clerk, failed disciple, dismissed analyst, minor investor sees the proof before the institution does.
- **Reward**: emotional bond, anticipation, underdog trust.
- **Proof object**: saved life, note, unofficial record, private bid.
- **Fresh variation**: the weak witness later becomes the key to public recognition.
- **Failure mode**: early witness has no future function.

## Card Upgrade Checklist

For every selected card:

- Assign a scene job.
- Define proof object.
- Define witness.
- Define cost.
- Define how it repeats once with variation.
- Mark the card as restricted if it risks copying a known work.
