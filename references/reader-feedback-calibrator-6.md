# Reader Feedback Calibrator 6

Use this file when the request asks for "고도화 진행6", reader reaction calibration, retention risk, fatigue prediction, comment simulation, paid-trust repair, or whether a selected cliche portfolio will keep working after the first proof.

Phase 6 upgrades the engine from "choose a strong cliche portfolio" to "predict how readers will react to the portfolio and repair the first likely drop-off point." It assumes earlier phases can compile and rank cliches, then stress-tests them through reader expectation, satisfaction, curiosity, fatigue, and trust.

## Reference Basis

Do not copy examples from external references. Extract abstract response mechanics:

| Reference family | What to extract | Skill use |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Narrative engagement research | curiosity, suspense, surprise, transportation, emotional investment | classify what keeps the next click alive |
| Cliffhanger and seriality studies | unresolved question, delayed payoff, rhythm of answer and new problem | separate fair paid hooks from empty withholding |

| Korean webnovel practice | fast genre recognition, visible reward, public reaction, paid boundary | localize reader comments and drop-off risks |

## Feedback Model

Predict four response bands.

| Band | Reader question | Positive signal | Risk signal | Repair target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| click | "무슨 재미인지 바로 보이나?" | title/opening promise is recognizable | genre promise is hidden by lore | sharpen identity, advantage, first pressure |
| first proof | "주인공이 왜 다른지 보이나?" | visible proof, witness, small payoff | private power-up, no reaction | add object, metric, public procedure, witness |
| repeat | "다음 화도 같은 재미가 다르게 나오나?" | method repeats under changed pressure | same reversal repeats | change witness, proof type, institution, or cost |
| paid trust | "돈 내도 답을 받을 것 같나?" | free section pays one reward and opens fair pressure | pure cliffhanger or delayed answer | pay before hook, name concrete next question |

## Reader Cohorts

Simulate at least three reader cohorts for substantial outputs.

| Cohort | Wants | Complains about | Best repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| speed reader | fast reward, clear hierarchy, visible win | slow lore, unclear rules | move proof earlier |
| genre loyalist | familiar promise delivered cleanly | fake novelty, wrong genre priority | restore genre reward |
| novelty seeker | one fresh pressure or moral angle | copy-paste opening, flat rivals | change institution, witness, or cost |
| power fantasy reader | agency, competence, public recognition | passive luck, nerf disguised as cost | make advantage active |
| strategy reader | clever method and fair setup | arbitrary future knowledge or cheating | add evidence trail and constraint |
| relationship/status reader | social recognition and changed access | private gains only | add public witness and status shift |
| paid-boundary reader | payoff before new hook | withheld answers | pay one promise before cliff |

Do not overfit to one cohort unless the brief names a specific audience.

## Response Signals

Use concrete, Korean webnovel-style reactions.

Positive signals:

- "이건 뭔 재미인지 바로 알겠다."
- "주인공이 직접 뭘 해서 이긴다."
- "주변 반응이 시원하다."
- "다음 화에서 어떤 식으로 또 증명할지 궁금하다."
- "익숙한데 증거물/목격자/비용이 달라서 덜 복붙 같다."
- "유료 넘어가도 바로 보상 하나는 줄 것 같다."

Complaint signals:

- "설정은 많은데 아직 재미가 안 보인다."
- "또 회귀/시스템인데 똑같다."
- "주인공이 운으로만 먹는다."
- "악역이 너무 멍청해서 시원함이 약하다."
- "보상이 혼자만 아는 거라 반응이 없다."
- "클리프행어만 있고 받은 게 없다."
- "장르가 섞였는데 첫 클릭 약속이 흐려졌다."

## Feedback Axes

Score 0-5 when a diagnosis or phase-6 artifact is needed.

| Axis | 5 means | 0 means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| promise clarity | genre/reward understood in one line | premise requires lore explanation |
| first reward visibility | reward appears through object, number, witness, status, money, access, or changed rule | reward remains internal |
| agency belief | protagonist's action earns the result | result feels gifted or arbitrary |
| reaction pleasure | witness response multiplies payoff | no one meaningful reacts |
| repeat curiosity | method can repeat with altered pressure | only one reveal exists |
| freshness credibility | variation changes scene pressure | novelty is only terminology |
| fatigue risk | cliche remains stable for 5+ beats | repetition becomes stale by episode 2 |
| paid-trust safety | payoff happens before new hook | hook withholds the first promised reward |
| confusion control | rules are clear enough for pleasure | terms and institutions bury the fun |
| copy-shadow safety | no recognizable source chain remains | scene order/rules/institutions resemble a named work |

Verdict:

| Average | Verdict | Meaning |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 4.3-5.0 | pass | ready or needs only wording polish |
| 3.4-4.2 | revise | usable but one risk should be repaired |
| 0-3.3 | rebuild | core promise, proof, or trust is weak |

## Episode Feedback Map

For a 1-5 loop, predict likely response by episode.

| Episode | Must pay | Must open | Common fatigue risk | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | title promise, first proof, witness reaction | concrete new pressure | setup delays proof | start with pressure and proof object |
| 2 | same method under new condition | institution notices | repeated face-slap | change proof type or witness |
| 3 | social/institutional consequence | stronger opponent or rule | reaction too easy | add competent authority |
| 4 | cost caused by success | bigger arena | random punishment | make cost logically caused by win |
| 5 | mini-arc payoff | paid-boundary question | pure withholding | answer one promise, then open a larger one |

## Paid-Trust Rule

The paid boundary should use:

```text
payoff -> recognition -> cost revealed -> concrete next question
```

Avoid:

```text
setup -> withheld answer -> unrelated threat -> "next time"
```

Fair hook examples:

- a guild publicly recognizes the protagonist, then files a contract dispute;
- a board accepts the acquisition, then reveals a hidden poison pill;
- a sect elder grants access, then names the taboo cost;
- a ranking board updates, then flags the score as impossible;
- a social circle accepts the heroine, then a legal claim attacks her title.

## Output Contract

For phase-6 outputs, include:

- reference families used;
- reader cohorts simulated;
- feedback axis scores;
- likely positive comments;
- likely complaints;
- episode feedback map;
- fatigue risk;

- copy-shadow risk;
- first repair;
- revised proof, loop, or hook after repair;
- final verdict.

## Repair Priority

Repair in this order:

1. promise clarity;
2. first reward visibility;
3. protagonist agency;
4. witness reaction;
5. repeat curiosity;
6. paid-trust safety;
7. freshness credibility;
8. long-term fatigue.

Do not repair weak reader response by adding lore. Repair by changing pressure, proof object, witness, cost, or payoff order.
