# Reference Triangulation Protocol 3

Use this file for the third upgrade layer: when a cliche has been gathered from many references and must be verified before it enters a trope engine, concept bible, or episode roadmap.

Triangulation means a cliche is not accepted because one source mentions it. It must be useful from at least three angles:

1. **Recognition**: readers can recognize the pleasure quickly.
2. **Structure**: the cliche performs a repeatable story function.
3. **Commercial Korean webnovel fit**: it can be staged through Korean webnovel institutions, rewards, witnesses, and hooks.

## Reference Classes

| Class | Examples | What it proves | What it cannot prove |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Trope catalogue | TV Tropes-like indexes, trope lists, genre tag pages | audience-recognizable patterns | commercial fit in Korean serial form |
| Motif / folklore index | tale type, motif index, donor tests, forbidden rooms, tokens | deep reusable motif units | platform-market usefulness |
| Structural theory | Propp functions, dramatic situations, three-act, kishotenketsu | sequence, pressure, turn, recognition | current reader taste |
| Web fiction genre discourse | progression fantasy, LitRPG, xianxia, isekai, transmigration tags | measurable growth and serial loop logic | Korean localization by itself |
| Korean webnovel studies | regression, reincarnation, possession, system motifs | Korean desire codes and genre grammar | specific current platform ranking |
| Platform/tag observation | Naver Series, KakaoPage, Munpia, WebNovel, Royal Road tags | visible genre labels and browsing signals | literary quality or future trend certainty |

## Triangulation Score

Score each cliche from 0 to 2 on each axis.

| Axis | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | hard to understand | genre readers may recognize | obvious within one line |
| Story function | decorative | has one scene use | creates repeatable sequence |
| Reward clarity | vague mood | one reward | reward plus proof object |
| Korean localization | generic foreign surface | partly localized | institution/witness/proof are Korean webnovel-ready |
| Serial durability | one-off | short arc | repeatable across arcs |
| Originality safety | close to a work | generic but has echoes | safely abstracted |
| Remixability | fixed | one variation | multiple operators can change it |

**Pass**: 11-14  
**Revise**: 8-10  
**Reject or rebuild**: 0-7

## Minimum Evidence Rule

Before adding a new cliche to a stable bank, attach at least three supports:

- one **recognition support**: trope catalogue, platform tag, common genre label, or reader-facing convention;
- one **structure support**: function, motif, dramatic situation, or beat structure;
- one **localization support**: Korean webnovel genre engine, institution, reward, proof scene, or platform category.

Do not use this to cite exact scenes. Use it to justify the cliche's function.

## Triangulation Workflow

1. **Name the cliche in generic terms**.
2. **Map reference support**:
   - recognition support;
   - structure support;
   - localization support.
3. **Convert to cliche schema**:
   - role;
   - reward;
   - proof object;
   - witness;
   - cost;
   - repeat loop.
4. **Apply one remix operator** if the card is too familiar.
5. **Run graph necessity**: remove this card from the concept. If nothing changes, the card is decorative.
6. **Run copy-risk check**: remove named terms, scene order, unique rules, and signature relationship layouts.
7. **Give verdict**: pass, revise, reject.

## Reference Confidence Tags

Use these tags in internal planning or outputs when useful:

- `stable-reference`: motif, structure, or broad genre grammar unlikely to change quickly.
- `platform-signal`: observed platform/tag/category signal; useful but can change.
- `research-backed`: from study or paper, but still needs webnovel localization.
- `market-volatile`: depends on current rankings or newest trend; browse before claiming.
- `copy-risk`: too close to a known work until abstracted.
- `scene-ready`: can be staged immediately with pressure, action, proof, witness, and cost.

## Repair Recipes

### Recognition is weak.

- Add a genre label, rank, contract, gate, tower floor, sect trial, board vote, social ceremony, or death flag.

### Structure is weak.

- Convert the cliche into one of these functions: test, donor aid, forbidden access, exposure, pursuit, rescue, recognition, return, punishment.

### Korean localization is weak.

- Move the cliche into a local institution: association, guild, system, constellation, chaebol board, sect, academy, court, market, media, household.

### Serial durability is weak.

- Attach the cliche to a loop: pressure -> action -> proof -> witness -> reward -> cost -> stronger pressure.

### Originality safety is weak.

- Change at least four of these: institution, proof object, protagonist action, scene order, cost, witness, antagonist motive.

## Final Rule

Do not accept a reference-derived cliche unless it can answer:

- What will readers recognize?
- What scene function does it perform?
- Who measures it?
- What visible proof pays the reward?
- What new pressure keeps the serial alive?
