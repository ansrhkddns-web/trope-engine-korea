# Product Packaging

Use this file when generating or diagnosing titles, loglines, introductions, tags, opening promises, first-screen hooks, and free-to-paid alignment. This is evergreen packaging guidance, not live market research.

If the user asks what is trending today on a platform, use `market-research-protocol.md` first.

## Package Contract

The title, intro, tags, opening scene, and first 5 episodes should sell the same promise.

- **Title**: names the fantasy or contradiction.
- **Intro/logline**: states the protagonist, advantage, and repeated fun.
- **Tags**: help readers categorize the promise.
- **Opening scene**: proves the promise quickly.
- **First 5 episodes**: prove the fun repeats.
- **Paid boundary**: pays one promise before opening a bigger one.

## Title Patterns

Use title patterns as structural prompts, not formulas to copy.

| Pattern | Function | Example shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role reversal | shows status fantasy | "버려진 [역할]이 [상위 지위]가 됐다" |
| Advantage claim | sells protagonist's edge | "[능력/정보]로 [장르 보상]을 독식한다" |
| Institution contradiction | creates freshness | "[기관]이 모르는 [가치]" |
| Future knowledge | promises regression payoff | "회귀자는 [미래 자산/인물/사건]을 선점한다" |
| Support class reversal | sells underdog utility | "[비전투 직업]인데 [핵심 보상]을 지배한다" |
| Revenge engine | sells catharsis | "[나를 버린/망친 대상]의 [권력]을 빼앗는다" |
| Rule exploit | sells cleverness | "[시스템/탑/문파]의 허점을 이용한다" |

## Title Quality Gate

Check:

- Can readers identify the genre reward immediately?
- Does the title imply a repeatable engine, not a one-time twist?
- Does it name the protagonist's advantage or reversal?
- Is it too abstract, poetic, or lore-heavy?
- Does it overpromise a reward the opening does not deliver?

## Logline Formula

Use:

`[불리한 출발점]의 주인공이 [우위/정보/능력]으로 [기관/장르 무대]에서 [반복 보상]을 얻고, [더 큰 압박]으로 확장된다.`

Examples of fields:

- **불리한 출발점**: F급, 버려진 후계자, 외문 제자, 죽을 예정인 엑스트라, 무시당한 지원직.
- **우위**: 회귀 기억, 감정 능력, 숨은 제작식, 계약 허점, 기연의 진짜 가격.
- **기관/무대**: 헌터 협회, 재벌 이사회, 탑 튜토리얼, 사문, 아카데미, 길드 시장.
- **반복 보상**: 랭크 상승, 돈, 복수, 구조, 스카우트, 듀얼 승리, 지분 확보.
- **더 큰 압박**: 기관의 통제, 미래 변경, 후원자 전쟁, 가문 내전, 문파 재판.

## Tag Clusters

Choose 5-10 tags that match actual promise. Do not add tags only because they sound popular.

- **헌터/게이트**: 헌터, 게이트, 던전, 길드, 랭킹, 성장, 레이드, 시스템
- **탑/시스템**: 탑등반, 시스템, 상태창, 히든조건, 성좌, 랭커, 튜토리얼
- **회귀/정보**: 회귀, 미래지식, 선점, 복수, 재도전, 운명개변
- **재벌/기업**: 재벌, 주식, 부동산, 기업인수, 이사회, 경영, 복수
- **무협**: 무협, 사문, 마교, 정파, 기연, 무공, 비급, 복수
- **아카데미**: 아카데미, 랭킹, 시험, 라이벌, 팀전, 성장
- **빙의/환생**: 빙의, 환생, 엑스트라, 악역, 생존, 원작개변
- **지원직/제작**: 제작, 감정, 힐러, 포터, 연금, 물류, 아이템

## Intro Checklist

The intro should include:

- protagonist's bad starting point;
- unfair advantage;
- first arena;
- visible repeated reward;
- pressure that keeps the plot moving;
- one fresh angle.

Avoid:

- long lore before the fantasy;
- proper nouns that mean nothing to new readers;
- vague phrases like "a grand destiny begins";
- promising revenge, money, or rank without showing how it repeats.

## First 5 Episode Package

| Episode | Package job | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | prove title promise | first visible reward and hook |
| 2 | show method is repeatable | second use of advantage with variation |
| 3 | add witness/social proof | institution or important person reacts |
| 4 | reveal cost or enemy | win creates pressure |
| 5 | deliver first mini-arc payoff | reward plus bigger hook |

## Paid Boundary

At a free-to-paid turn:

- free final episode should show the reward is close or partly paid;
- first paid episode should pay something concrete quickly;
- then open the next larger problem;
- never make payment feel like only a withheld answer.

## Package Diagnosis

Mark mismatch if:

- title promises money but first episodes only show combat;
- tags promise revenge but no wrong or evidence is visible;
- intro promises intelligence but protagonist only receives gifts;
- opening explains setting but does not prove the fun;
- paid hook withholds without paying.

Repair by aligning title, intro, first proof, and first 5 episode rewards around one primary promise.
