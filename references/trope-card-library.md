# Trope Card Library

Use these cards as modular pieces. Each card should pay a reader reward and create a next event.

## Index

- `Rank / Measurement Cards`: rank test, public ranking, hidden title.
- `System / Tower / Gate Cards`: tutorial, hidden condition, gate crisis, sponsor bid.
- `Regression / Information Cards`: future assets, recruitment, disaster preemption, traitor knowledge.
- `Social Reversal Cards`: humiliation reversal, VIP offer, bureaucratic loophole.
- `Finance / Chaebol Cards`: boardroom reversal, takeover, land, crisis investment.
- `Martial Arts Cards`: broken meridian, fortuitous encounter, hypocrisy, duel.
- `Production / Support Class Cards`: crafting, healing, appraisal, logistics.
- `Revenge / Evidence Cards`: receipts, legal/social punishment, delayed revenge.
- `Emotional Bond Cards`: loyalty, debt, rescued future enemy, family reframe.
- `Episode Hook Cards`: rank, choice, name, cost, witness, market reaction.

## Card Format

- **Function**: what the trope does in the story.
- **Basic form**: the familiar version readers recognize.
- **Reward**: what pleasure it pays.
- **Variation**: how to make it feel fresh.
- **Risk**: how it becomes stale.

## Rank / Measurement Cards

### Awakening or Rank Test Error

- **Function**: prove the protagonist is mismeasured by the current institution.
- **Basic form**: the measuring device says F-rank, error, unreadable, or impossible value.
- **Reward**: humiliation reversal, hidden value, official reaction.
- **Variation**: device is not wrong; it measures the wrong category. Insurance grade, rescue value, dungeon compatibility, legal hazard, or market value is abnormal.
- **Risk**: repeated "the device exploded" with no new consequence.

### Public Ranking Shock

- **Function**: turn private growth into social proof.
- **Basic form**: ranking board updates and everyone notices.
- **Reward**: recognition, envy, guild interest, enemy panic.
- **Variation**: ranking is segmented by rescue count, market value, kill efficiency, survival rate, viewer trust, or sponsor debt.
- **Risk**: rank number changes but no one important reacts.

### Hidden Title / Hidden Class

- **Function**: give a compact identity and future reward path.
- **Basic form**: hidden class appears after impossible condition.
- **Reward**: exclusivity, growth potential, reader speculation.
- **Variation**: the title creates obligations, taxes, enemies, contracts, or public misunderstandings.
- **Risk**: too many titles with no scene function.

## System / Tower / Gate Cards

### Tutorial Exploit

- **Function**: prove the protagonist understands rules better than the system expects.
- **Basic form**: uses future knowledge to clear a tutorial condition.
- **Reward**: intelligence, first reward, rank shock.
- **Variation**: exploit is legal but socially scandalous; it saves weak players; it creates a precedent administrators must address.
- **Risk**: exploit feels like a cheat code instead of a choice.

### Hidden Condition Clear

- **Function**: convert observation into reward.
- **Basic form**: protagonist notices the real condition is not "kill all monsters."
- **Reward**: cleverness, rare item, system recognition.
- **Variation**: condition is emotional, legal, economic, ecological, or tied to protecting someone.
- **Risk**: hidden condition is arbitrary.

### Dungeon Break / Gate Crisis

- **Function**: force action before the protagonist is officially ready.
- **Basic form**: low-level gate mutates or breaks into public space.
- **Reward**: rescue, status proof, public witness, enemy embarrassment.
- **Variation**: crisis happens during audit, livestream, family event, board meeting, school exam, land inspection, or sect ceremony.
- **Risk**: disaster has no cost after the hero wins.

### Sponsor or Constellation Bid

- **Function**: make higher powers react to protagonist value.
- **Basic form**: constellations offer coins, skills, or contracts.
- **Reward**: recognition, negotiation, cosmic scale.
- **Variation**: sponsors have portfolios, reputational risk, contract clauses, fan wars, or incompatible values.
- **Risk**: sponsors become joke comments instead of plot actors.

## Regression / Information Cards

### Future Asset Arbitrage

- **Function**: make information advantage visible through money or ownership.
- **Basic form**: buys stock, land, item, or skill before its value rises.
- **Reward**: competence, wealth, revenge setup.
- **Variation**: acquisition requires social proof, legal cover, debt, sacrifice, or a public reason that hides future knowledge.
- **Risk**: free money without obstacle.

### Recruit Future Giant

- **Function**: turn knowledge into relationship capital.
- **Basic form**: protagonist finds a future ranker, genius, doctor, idol, or strategist early.
- **Reward**: loyalty, team growth, reader anticipation.
- **Variation**: the future giant is currently a liability, criminal suspect, failed trainee, enemy child, or ideological opposite.
- **Risk**: recruited people become trophies with no agency.

### Disaster Preemption

- **Function**: prove regression knowledge while creating altered future tension.
- **Basic form**: protagonist prevents a known tragedy.
- **Reward**: rescue, moral satisfaction, reputation.
- **Variation**: saving one event creates a new crisis, exposes the protagonist, or changes the antagonist's timing.
- **Risk**: future knowledge solves every problem cleanly.

### Traitor Knowledge

- **Function**: create suspense between what the protagonist knows and what others believe.
- **Basic form**: protagonist knows who will betray whom.
- **Reward**: revenge, tension, dramatic irony.
- **Variation**: traitor has a valid grievance; betrayal can be redirected; public exposure would harm an innocent.
- **Risk**: traitor is cartoonish and the reveal is too easy.

## Social Reversal Cards

### Humiliation-to-Proof Scene

- **Function**: turn disrespect into a satisfying reversal.
- **Basic form**: family, guild, sect, class, or board dismisses protagonist; protagonist proves value.
- **Reward**: vindication, status jump, public reaction.
- **Variation**: the proof saves the humiliator, creates debt, or forces an institution to break its own rule.
- **Risk**: humiliation is too long or too cruel before payoff.

### VIP Scout / Forced Offer

- **Function**: show market recognition of protagonist value.
- **Basic form**: guild master, CEO, elder, sponsor, or professor offers a contract.
- **Reward**: social proof, leverage, choice.
- **Variation**: every offer has a hidden cost; refusing is more impressive than accepting; contracts conflict with moral goals.
- **Risk**: protagonist collects offers but does not choose.

### Bureaucratic Loophole

- **Function**: let cleverness beat raw power.
- **Basic form**: protagonist uses rules against the institution.
- **Reward**: intelligence, legal revenge, comedy, public shock.
- **Variation**: loophole involves hunter insurance, tower contracts, sect law, inheritance clauses, guild licenses, or auction rules.
- **Risk**: rules feel invented only for the scene.

## Finance / Chaebol Cards

### Boardroom Reversal

- **Function**: make business power visible as social domination.
- **Basic form**: protagonist is mocked in a meeting and reveals decisive leverage.
- **Reward**: status, revenge, competence.
- **Variation**: leverage is a tiny contract, land right, patent clause, creditor position, or guild license no one valued.
- **Risk**: villains only yell; no strategic counterplay.

### Hostile Takeover

- **Function**: transform accumulated assets into a major status jump.
- **Basic form**: protagonist quietly gathers shares and flips control.
- **Reward**: power, revenge, public headline.
- **Variation**: combine with dungeon resources, item patents, hunter guild shares, logistics chokepoints, or family debt.
- **Risk**: acquisition is too technical without emotional stakes.

### Land Before Announcement

- **Function**: give regression knowledge a simple visible reward.
- **Basic form**: protagonist buys land before development/gate/tower announcement.
- **Reward**: wealth, foresight, family reversal.
- **Variation**: land is dangerous, politically sensitive, haunted by gate residue, or needed to save people.
- **Risk**: pure speculation with no story conflict.

### Crisis Investment

- **Function**: make protagonist look bold when everyone else panics.
- **Basic form**: buys during crash, disaster, scandal, or dungeon panic.
- **Reward**: competence, profit, public contrast.
- **Variation**: investment also rescues workers, gains monopoly rights, or creates moral suspicion.
- **Risk**: market turns exactly as expected every time.

## Martial Arts Cards

### Broken Meridian Workaround

- **Function**: turn weakness into a unique cultivation path.
- **Basic form**: protagonist cannot train normally but finds another method.
- **Reward**: underdog growth, technique novelty.
- **Variation**: workaround uses medicine, contracts, demonic law, formation engineering, body economics, or system quantification.
- **Risk**: disability vanishes too easily.

### Fortuitous Encounter With a Price

- **Function**: give a major power jump and long-term debt.
- **Basic form**: cave manual, old master, relic, pill, or hidden realm.
- **Reward**: secret power, destiny, reader thrill.
- **Variation**: the encounter is legally owned, cursed, politically incriminating, or tied to an enemy's inheritance.
- **Risk**: random reward with no earned discovery.

### Orthodox Hypocrisy Exposure

- **Function**: create moral satisfaction and faction conflict.
- **Basic form**: righteous sect acts corrupt while demonic/outsider is more honest.
- **Reward**: anger, revenge, ideological twist.
- **Variation**: hypocrisy is bureaucratic, financial, medical, inheritance-based, or caused by survival pressure.
- **Risk**: all orthodox characters become identical villains.

### Duel Upset

- **Function**: provide clear public proof.
- **Basic form**: protagonist defeats stronger-ranked opponent through technique, knowledge, or rule use.
- **Reward**: status jump, crowd reaction, rival creation.
- **Variation**: victory condition is not killing but saving face, exposing poison, winning a contract, or forcing an elder's acknowledgment.
- **Risk**: duels repeat without new stakes.

## Production / Support Class Cards

### Undervalued Production Class

- **Function**: turn a "non-combat" role into hidden strategic value.
- **Basic form**: crafter, appraiser, healer, porter, cook, pharmacist, formation engineer, or logistics worker is dismissed.
- **Reward**: competence reversal, money, team dependence, public recognition.
- **Variation**: the support skill controls raid survival rate, item patent value, sect medicine supply, tower floor logistics, or guild insurance premiums.
- **Risk**: support role becomes secretly strongest in combat too quickly, erasing the initial appeal.

### Impossible Appraisal

- **Function**: let knowledge and perception create immediate value.
- **Basic form**: protagonist identifies an item/manual/land/gate others mispriced.
- **Reward**: knowledge superiority, money, social proof.
- **Variation**: appraisal is legally dangerous, morally suspicious, or exposes a hidden owner.
- **Risk**: repeated bargain hunting without conflict.

### Emergency Healer Proof

- **Function**: prove value through rescue and emotional response.
- **Basic form**: protagonist heals or stabilizes someone others gave up on.
- **Reward**: safety, gratitude, reputation, institutional dependence.
- **Variation**: healing has a visible cost, reveals corruption, creates a medical patent, or violates sect/association rules.
- **Risk**: healing becomes unlimited and removes stakes.

### Logistics Saves the Raid

- **Function**: make preparation and intelligence feel dramatic.
- **Basic form**: protagonist packed, routed, insured, or supplied correctly before a crisis.
- **Reward**: competence, rescue, guild dependence.
- **Variation**: logistics determines land value, guild valuation, sect war supplies, tower floor monopoly, or disaster evacuation routes.
- **Risk**: planning is explained instead of tested under pressure.

## Revenge / Evidence Cards

### Receipt Ledger

- **Function**: make revenge feel earned through accumulated proof.
- **Basic form**: protagonist gathers evidence of betrayal, embezzlement, poison, false accusation, or family abuse.
- **Reward**: anger payoff, legal/social reversal, catharsis.
- **Variation**: evidence is useful only if revealed at the right public venue: board vote, sect trial, guild hearing, livestream, auction, or rank exam.
- **Risk**: revenge is delayed too long without intermediate progress.

### Public Punishment Without Murder

- **Function**: create satisfying consequence while preserving larger conflict.
- **Basic form**: enemy loses rank, contract, face, shares, disciple, sponsor, license, or inheritance.
- **Reward**: revenge, status, moral satisfaction.
- **Variation**: punishment forces the enemy to survive as a future witness, informant, debtor, or institutional crack.
- **Risk**: punishment feels small if the enemy's loss is not visible.

### Villain Trap Reversal

- **Function**: let the enemy's scheme become the protagonist's proof scene.
- **Basic form**: enemy sets a trap; protagonist uses it to expose them.
- **Reward**: intelligence, revenge, public reaction.
- **Variation**: protagonist cannot reveal all knowledge, so they must win through partial evidence or bait.
- **Risk**: enemy looks foolish if the trap is too obvious.

## Emotional Bond Cards

### Rescue Creates Debt

- **Function**: convert action into relationship capital.
- **Basic form**: protagonist saves a future ally, enemy, family member, disciple, or civilian.
- **Reward**: safety, loyalty, reputation.
- **Variation**: the rescued person cannot openly repay, or their gratitude creates political danger.
- **Risk**: rescued characters become passive followers.

### Future Enemy as Early Dependent

- **Function**: create tension between knowledge and present emotion.
- **Basic form**: a person known as a future villain is currently vulnerable or useful.
- **Reward**: suspense, moral choice, relationship complexity.
- **Variation**: the protagonist can redirect the villain's path but must sacrifice an easy future advantage.
- **Risk**: the future villain becomes harmless too quickly.

### Family Reframe

- **Function**: refresh family/chaebol/sect humiliation by changing one relationship.
- **Basic form**: a family or sect member who ignored the protagonist must reassess them.
- **Reward**: status reversal, emotional satisfaction.
- **Variation**: the first person to believe is not the obvious ally, or belief creates a new succession/faction problem.
- **Risk**: all relatives are identical antagonists.

## Episode Hook Cards

### Changed Rank Hook

- End on a rank, title, share price, floor, realm, or family standing changing in public.

### Forced Choice Hook

- End when protagonist must accept/refuse a contract, duel, rescue, bid, sponsor, inheritance, or betrayal.

### Name Reveal Hook

- End when a future enemy, sponsor, hidden master, buyer, family elder, or traitor name appears.

### Cost Reveal Hook

- End after reward is visible, then reveal the price.

### Witness Arrival Hook

- End when the person who should not have seen the event arrives.

### Market Reaction Hook

- End on stock surge, item auction panic, guild bid war, rumor explosion, or media headline.
