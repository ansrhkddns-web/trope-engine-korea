# Market Research Protocol

Use this only when the user asks for current platform trends, recent popular cliches, live ranking signals, contest direction, or platform-specific packaging. Current market data changes, so do not rely on memory.

## Research Targets

Prefer sources that show current reader-facing packaging:

- platform rankings and genre charts
- official contest pages and winner lists
- title, intro, tags, covers, and free-episode summaries
- reader reviews and comments when accessible
- publisher/platform promotional pages
- author notes only when they describe market positioning

## Sampling

- For a quick scan, inspect 10-20 visible titles in the target genre.
- For a stronger market map, inspect 30-50 titles across ranking, new, completed, and promotion lists.
- Separate platform signal from genre signal. A platform may overrepresent certain packaging styles.
- Record dates and platform names when summarizing.

## What to Extract

For each sampled work, extract only generalized signals:

- title promise pattern
- protagonist starting condition
- primary genre device
- secondary genre flavor
- first reward implied by title/intro
- status, money, power, revenge, romance, or survival reward
- unique-looking twist axis
- common tags or keywords
- paid/free transition hints if visible

Do not copy exact scenes, unique names, proprietary settings, or detailed plot beats. Convert observations into abstract trope grammar.

## Applying Research

After research, update the requested output by:

- keeping evergreen genre defaults from `default-genre-settings.md`;
- adding current packaging signals from the sample;
- marking which suggestions are stable genre grammar and which are current-market inferences;
- avoiding claims like "this is guaranteed to sell."
