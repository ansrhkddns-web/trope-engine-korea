# Scene Proof Bank

Use this file when a trope pack is too abstract. A good cliche becomes commercial only when it turns into a proof scene.

## Proof Scene Formula

Use this order:

1. **Pressure**: someone dismisses, threatens, tests, prices, audits, ranks, or traps the protagonist.
2. **Action**: the protagonist uses their advantage in a visible choice.
3. **Proof object**: something measurable changes.
4. **Witness reaction**: a person or institution must reassess value.
5. **Cost or hook**: the win opens a sharper problem.

## Scene Banks

### Rank Test With Mismeasurement

- **Pressure**: the protagonist is treated as disposable before an official test.
- **Action**: they choose a method the test was not built to measure.
- **Proof object**: the device returns an abnormal category, not just a big number.
- **Witness**: examiner, guild scout, rival, reporter, or association auditor.
- **Hook**: the result triggers a manual review, contract trap, or hidden authority alert.

### Hidden Dungeon Condition

- **Pressure**: everyone assumes the clear condition is combat.
- **Action**: protagonist reads environment, monster behavior, rule wording, or past/future clue.
- **Proof object**: quest clear message, hidden reward, rescued NPC, changed gate grade.
- **Witness**: party member, livestream audience, guild analyst, sponsor.
- **Hook**: clearing reveals that someone intentionally hid the condition.

### Boardroom Reversal

- **Pressure**: family or executives mock the protagonist's lack of authority.
- **Action**: protagonist reveals one small but decisive contract, vote, debt, land, patent, or timing move.
- **Proof object**: board vote flips, share price changes, acquisition clause activates.
- **Witness**: family head, rival heir, legal counsel, reporter, outside investor.
- **Hook**: the win exposes a larger patron behind the enemy.

### Fortuitous Encounter With Ownership

- **Pressure**: protagonist is sent to a worthless, dangerous, or humiliating place.
- **Action**: they identify the hidden value and choose whether to claim it.
- **Proof object**: manual, relic, medicine, formation, title, or realm reaction.
- **Witness**: hidden elder, enemy scout, future ally, sect record.
- **Hook**: the encounter belongs to someone, requires debt, or marks the protagonist.

### Support Class Saves Combatants

- **Pressure**: combatants ignore the support worker's warning.
- **Action**: protagonist prepares route, antidote, food, repair, appraisal, or insurance logic.
- **Proof object**: survival rate, stabilized injury, item upgrade, avoided wipeout.
- **Witness**: raid leader, healer, insurer, guild scout, rescued high-rank hunter.
- **Hook**: guilds start bidding while the original team tries to suppress the evidence.

### Revenge Evidence Turn

- **Pressure**: enemy frames the protagonist or repeats an old abuse pattern.
- **Action**: protagonist lets part of the trap happen to secure proof.
- **Proof object**: document, recording, poison trace, audit result, duel-law contradiction.
- **Witness**: judge, elder, board, association inspector, students, media.
- **Hook**: the exposed enemy names a higher sponsor or uses one final contract clause.

## First Episode Proof Checklist

Episode 1 should answer:

- What genre am I reading?
- Why is this protagonist fun?
- What reward did I just receive?
- Who saw the proof?
- What changed because of the proof?
- What do I want to click next to see?

If any answer is vague, rewrite the first proof scene before adding more setting.

## Scene Upgrade Moves

- Replace explanation with a test, bid, duel, audit, rescue, broadcast, contract, ranking, or hearing.
- Add a named witness with something to lose.
- Add a proof object that can be shown again later.
- Let the reward create a concrete cost.
- End before the social reaction fully resolves.
